-------------------------- List 10 questions that need AI --------------------------------

1. Can you create some realistic group poses based on our photo and the style we want?
Reason - AI can use our photo and preferences to create posing ideas that suit our group.

2. Can you tell if this email is a scam or not?
Reason - AI can look for suspicious words, unusual patterns, and signs of phishing.

3. Can you help me summarize this 50-page research paper?
Reason - AI can go through a long document and pick out the most important information.

4. What movies would you recommend based on what I've watched before?
Reason - AI can learn from my viewing history and suggest movies that match my interests.

5. Can you translate this technical document from English to Japanese without losing its meaning?
Reason - AI can understand and translate complicated sentences and technical terms better than translating word by word.

6. Can you turn this noisy recording into text, even though several people are talking at once?
Reason - AI can separate different voices and recognize speech even when there is background noise.

7. Can you predict what products customers might buy next?
Reason - AI can study past purchases and find patterns to predict what customers may want.

8. Can you make a realistic cooking video just from a written recipe?
Reason - AI can create new video scenes based on the recipe without needing existing footage.

9. Can AI detect early signs of diseases from medical scans?
Reason - AI can spot small details and patterns in medical images that are hard to detect by human eyes.

10. Can you turn regular 2D photos into realistic 3D models?
Reason - AI can estimate the depth and shape of objects, including parts that aren't visible in the photos.


-------------------------- List 10 questions that don't need AI ---------------------------

1. What's 25 times 16?
Reason - A normal calculator can easily give the correct answer.

2. How many students have signed up for this class?
Reason - We can simply count the students in the database.

3. What time does this store close?
Reason - We can just check the store's opening hours.

4. Can you put these names in alphabetical order?
Reason - We can sort them by hand or use simple sorting algorithm.

5. Did my bank transfer go through this morning?
Reason - We only need to check the bank's transaction records to confirm it.

6. Does my password have at least 12 characters?
Reason - We can count the characters ourselves.

7. How many days are there between these two dates?
Reason - A date calculator can work out the difference.

8. How many meters are in 5 kilometers?
Reason - We can use a simple conversion formula to get the answer.

9. Is this username already taken?
Reason - We can check the database to see if someone is already using it.

10. How much do these three items cost altogether?
Reason - We just need to add up the prices of the three items.
