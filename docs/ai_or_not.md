-------------------------- List 10 questions that need AI --------------------------------

1. Can a system generate realistic posing examples for a group based on their uploaded photo and a natural language description? Reason - Requires generative AI to synthesize customized visual arrangements from image and text inputs.
2. Is this email potentially a phishing scam? Reason - AI can identify suspicious language, patterns, and contextual clues.
3. Can you summarize this 50-page research paper? Reason - AI can extract important information from lengthy, unstructured text.
4. Which movie would I enjoy based on my viewing history? Reason - AI can recognize preference patterns and personalize recommendations.
5. Can you translate this technical documentation from English into fluent Japanese? Reason - AI can translate complex phrasing and domain terminology better than literal word-for-word translation.
6. Can a system transcribe a conversation where several people speak simultaneously with background noise? Reason - Requires AI-based speech separation and recognition to interpret overlapping audio.
7. Can you predict which products customers are likely to buy next? Reason - AI can learn from historical data to estimate future behavior.
8. Can a system generate a realistic cooking video from a written recipe without using prerecorded footage? Reason - Requires generative AI to synthesize new and temporally coherent visual content.
9. Can a system detect early signs of disease from medical scans? Reason - AI identifies subtle visual patterns that cannot easily be defined using fixed rules.
10. Can a system automatically generate realistic 3D models from ordinary 2D photographs? Reason - AI infers depth, geometry, and hidden surfaces from incomplete visual information.


-------------------------- List 10 questions that don't need AI ---------------------------

1. What is 25 × 16? Reason - A basic calculator can provide an exact answer.
2. How many students are registered in this class? Reason - A database query can count the records.
3. What time does the store close according to its schedule? Reason - The answer can be retrieved directly from stored information.
4. Can you sort these names alphabetically? Reason - A standard sorting algorithm can perform this task.
5. Did my scheduled bank transfer go through this morning? Reason - Transactional verification requires secure ledger checks rather than AI inference.
6. Is my password at least 12 characters long? Reason - A simple rule can check the character count.
7. How many days are there between two dates? Reason - A date calculation function can determine the difference.
8. Can you convert 5 kilometers into meters? Reason - A fixed mathematical conversion gives the answer.
9. Is this username already registered? Reason - A database lookup can check whether it exists.
10. What is the total price of three items in my shopping cart? Reason - Basic arithmetic can calculate the total.
